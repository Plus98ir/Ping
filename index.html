How to read the numbers

Average ping matters less than jitter and loss. A steady 90 ms plays better than a 40 ms line that spikes.

Lowering your ping

In order of how much they actually help. The first three are worth more than everything below them combined.

Fixing packet loss

Packet loss is a fault, not a speed problem. Find where packets die, then fix that one hop.
